\section{Part E}
What is your conclusion about the sales person's claim?\\
-----\\

The t-statistic of $ 12.946 $ indicates that the observed difference in mean is nearly 13 standard errors away from zero. Pairing this result with a p-value of $ 1.6197 \times 10^{-31} $ suggests that if the null hypothesis were true, the probability of observing a t-statistic this high would be virtually zero. This provides overwhelming evidence in favor of rejecting the null hypothesis and concluding that the salesperson is correct in claiming Japanese car mileage is better than US car mileage.
